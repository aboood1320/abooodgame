## Full description

**Twenty board games in one polished Arabic app.**

Monopoly, Catan, Ticket to Ride, Carcassonne, Risk, Scrabble, Cluedo, Uno, Pandemic, Backgammon, Waraq (Basra & Rummy), Dominoes, Ludo, Snakes & Ladders, Checkers, Mancala, Love Letter, Chess, Koot Bo Sitta, and Splendor. Every game is built from scratch with a clear Arabic interface, a detailed rules guide, and three AI levels.

### Highlights

- **Complete Arabic rules for every game:** goal, components, setup, turn-by-turn play, and win conditions — inside the app.
- **Hotseat play:** 2–6 players on one device, with secret information truly hidden until “Reveal” is pressed.
- **Worldwide online rooms:** create a room, share a code or invite link, or use quick match. Connections are peer-to-peer and require no account.
- **Three AI levels:** easy, normal, and hard. Mix human and CPU players on the same table.
- **Per-game options:** hand size, stone count, target score, jokers, forced captures, and more — applied consistently in local and online play.
- **Works offline:** after the first visit the app is cached on your device and local play continues without a connection.
- **Autosave:** resume your last local match exactly where you left it.
- **Animation and sound:** cards flip, dice tumble, pieces glide, and results celebrate — with layered, varied audio and a reduced-motion option.
- **Live spectating:** anyone waiting for a seat watches the game update live, without seeing hidden hands.
- **No accounts, no ads, no tracking.**

### How online play works

1. Open **Online play** and choose **Create room**.
2. Pick the game, seat count, AI level, and game options.
3. Share the room code or invite link with friends.
4. Press **Start game** — the host keeps the authoritative state and each player sees only their own seat. Anyone waiting to join watches the match live as a spectator.

Rooms use direct WebRTC connections between browsers. On restrictive networks an optional TURN server can be configured in the network settings.

### Privacy in short

No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Settings and progress are stored locally on your device. A public signalling service (PeerJS) and STUN servers are used only to establish connections. See the privacy policy for details.

## Reviewer notes

- No account or sign-in is required, and no personal data is collected on our servers.
- To test online play: open two windows, create a room in the first, then paste the room code or invite link into the second.
- To verify offline mode: load the app once, disable the network, then reload — the library must appear and local games must keep working.
- All audio and art assets are local and CC0-licensed; no external asset CDNs are used.
- The install button appears automatically in Chrome/Edge once PWA criteria are met; on iOS, “Add to Home Screen” instructions are shown from the share menu.
